    Abidjan Gris Marble Effect Polished Porcelain Tile 30x60

    Transform your interiors with the elegant Abidjan Gris Polished Porcelain Tiles. Measuring a versatile 30x60 cm, these premium tiles are expertly crafted for both wall and floor applications, allowing you to create a seamless, unified look throughout your home. Featuring a stunning grey (Gris) marble effect, they deliver the luxurious aesthetic of natural stone combined with the outstanding durability and low maintenance of high-quality porcelain.

    The high-gloss polished finish beautifully reflects light, instantly brightening up kitchens, bathrooms, and living areas. Designed with modern precision, these tiles boast rectified edges, allowing for tighter, narrower grout lines and a sleek, contemporary finish. With 16 unique graphic faces, the Abidjan Gris collection ensures a highly authentic, varied marble pattern without obvious repetition. Packaged with 8 tiles per box, this is the ultimate choice for upgrading your space with a sophisticated, timeless design.
